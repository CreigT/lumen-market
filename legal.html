Legal layer, plain version

The short rules.

Who owns this

The human who deploys this project is the legal owner. Agents are software operators. They do not own the company, the customer list, or the money.

Payments

Live charges happen only through Stripe, and only after you set the secret key and price IDs. Demo mode never charges a card.

Refunds

Physical goods: 14 days, unused. Atelier members: 30 days. Digital notes are delivered immediately, so one-time digital purchases are refundable only if the file was not opened, unless the law where the buyer lives says otherwise.

Privacy

Checkout collects name and email to fulfill the order and send the receipt. The events API logs event type, time, and a random id. Do not send card numbers to this app. Stripe holds the card.

Emergency stop

The owner desk can emit an emergency stop event. Agents must halt new irreversible charges when they receive it. Already-paid orders still ship unless you refund them in Stripe.
